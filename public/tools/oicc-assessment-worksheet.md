## Rating method

- **Implemented:** The control objective is implemented and supported by current evidence.
- **Partially implemented:** Some elements exist, but the objective or evidence is incomplete.
- **Not implemented:** The objective is not presently achieved.
- **Not applicable:** Applicability was evaluated and the rationale was documented.
- **Not determined:** More examination, interview, or testing is required.
